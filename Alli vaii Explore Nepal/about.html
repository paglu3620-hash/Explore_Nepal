THE SOUL OF MITHILA

Discover the cultural heart of Janakpur

Janakpur Dham is a city where mythology, spirituality, art and everyday life beautifully come together. Known for its deep connection with the Ramayana and Mithila heritage, the city offers an experience unlike anywhere else in Nepal.

From the magnificent Janaki Mandir and sacred ponds to the colorful streets, traditional Mithila artwork and lively celebrations, Janakpur invites visitors to experience Nepal through its history and culture.

Janaki Mandir The iconic temple of Janakpur
Ramanand Chowk A vibrant landmark and gateway
Mithila Art Colorful traditional artistic heritage
Sacred Heritage Temples, ponds and ancient traditions
